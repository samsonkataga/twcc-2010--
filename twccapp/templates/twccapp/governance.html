Governance Framework

TWCC operates under a robust governance structure ensuring accountability, transparency, and effective leadership.

General Assembly

The supreme governing body comprising all members, convening annually to set strategic direction.

  • Annual Meetings
  • Strategic Approval
  • Constitutional Amendments

National Board

Elected representatives providing oversight and policy direction between assemblies.

  • Quarterly Meetings
  • Policy Formulation
  • Financial Oversight

Executive Committee

Operational leadership implementing board decisions and managing daily affairs.

  • Monthly Meetings
  • Program Implementation
  • Staff Supervision

Regional Chapters

Local governance structures adapting national programs to regional contexts.

  • Local Implementation
  • Community Engagement
  • Regional Advocacy
