Our Journey Through Time

Milestones that shaped our organization and impacted thousands of women entrepreneurs across Tanzania

Foundation

TWCC was established by a group of 12 visionary women entrepreneurs led by Fatuma Mwamba and Grace Mwakasege to address the systemic challenges faced by women in business across Tanzania. The founding meeting was held at the Dar es Salaam Serena Hotel with 35 attendees.

  • Initial membership of 87 women
  • First office in Kariakoo
  • Focus on micro-business support
2005 Established
2008 Expansion

First Regional Chapter

The launch of our Arusha chapter marked our first expansion beyond Dar es Salaam, establishing a model for nationwide outreach. This chapter became a blueprint for our regional expansion strategy.

Key Outcomes:

  • 150 new members in first 6 months
  • First regional business summit with 300 attendees
  • Partnership with Arusha City Council

Key Milestones

Significant achievements that demonstrate our growth and impact

2010 - Presidential Recognition

Received the National Business Excellence Award from President Jakaya Kikwete for outstanding contribution to women's economic empowerment.

2014 - International Expansion

Partnered with South Sudan Women Entrepreneurs Association to establish their first Women's Chamber of Commerce.

2018 - Headquarters

Inaugurated our permanent national headquarters in Dar es Salaam's central business district.
