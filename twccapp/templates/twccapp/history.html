Our History

The Journey of Empowering Women in Business

Our Journey Through Time

2005

Foundation

TWCC was established by a group of visionary women entrepreneurs to address the unique challenges faced by women in business across Tanzania.

2008

First Regional Chapter

Expanded operations with the launch of our first regional chapter in Arusha, marking the beginning of nationwide outreach.

2012

Policy Advocacy Milestone

Successfully advocated for the inclusion of gender-sensitive provisions in Tanzania's National Trade Policy.

2015

10th Anniversary

Celebrated a decade of service with over 5,000 members and recognition from the Tanzanian government for our contributions.

2020

Pandemic Response

Launched emergency support programs helping 2,000 women-led businesses survive the COVID-19 economic impact.

2023

Current Reach

Now serving 20,000+ members through 28 regional chapters with programs impacting over 1 million women nationwide.

Key Milestones

2010 - Presidential Recognition

Received national recognition for outstanding contribution to women's economic empowerment.

2014 - International Expansion

Assisted South Sudan in establishing their first Women's Chamber of Commerce.

2018 - Headquarters Establishment

Opened our permanent national headquarters in Dar es Salaam.
