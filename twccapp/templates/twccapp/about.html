Empowering Women Entrepreneurs

Tanzania Women Chamber of Commerce (TWCC) is the leading apex organization for women's economic empowerment in Tanzania, established in 2005. As the prima ry umbrella body for women in business across Tanzania, TWCC works to drive eco nomic growth, policy reform, and capacity-building initiatives for women entrepreneurs.

Our Mission

To create women’s enterprises which are competitive locally, regionally, and internationally through advocating and tapping into opportunities available.

Our Vision

A strong Apex Organization empowering women economically.
