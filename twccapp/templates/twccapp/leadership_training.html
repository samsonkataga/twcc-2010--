Leadership Training Program

Program Overview

Our 6-month leadership development program is designed to cultivate the next generation of African leaders through a comprehensive curriculum that combines theoretical knowledge with practical application.

Program Components

  • Core leadership theory and practice
  • One-on-one mentorship from industry leaders
  • Community project design and implementation
  • Networking opportunities with peers and professionals
  • Personal development workshops

Who Should Apply?

This program is ideal for young Africans aged 18-35 who demonstrate leadership potential and want to develop their skills to make a greater impact in their communities and careers.
